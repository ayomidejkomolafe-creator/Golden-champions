How to get booked in

There's no online booking. Everything is arranged directly with Victoria.

  1. Send your quote

    Use the quote builder or WhatsApp a photo of the style you want. Say the length and size.

  2. Agree a price and time

    Victoria quotes you and finds a slot, Monday to Saturday or a Sunday by appointment.

  3. Come to the salon

    1a Pier Road, Erith, DA8 1TA. No need to detangle first. Victoria preps your hair before she starts.
