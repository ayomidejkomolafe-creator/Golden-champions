How to get booked in

There's no online booking. Everything is arranged directly with Victoria.

  1. Send a message

    WhatsApp a photo of the style you want, or call. Say the length and size.

  2. Agree a price and time

    Victoria will quote you and find a slot, Monday to Saturday or a Sunday by appointment.

  3. Come to the salon

    1a Pier Road, Erith, DA8 1TA. No need to detangle your hair first. Victoria will prep it before she starts.
